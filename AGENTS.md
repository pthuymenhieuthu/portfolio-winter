# Portfolio content rules

- Use the user's supplied or approved copy. Do not invent additional claims, explanations, captions, slogans, or process descriptions.
- Keep small labels only when they are necessary to understand the content, illustration, navigation, or product status. Do not render build notes, draft markers, review instructions, or redundant summaries in the page.
- Preserve required factual labels such as “Prototype in development” and “Sample data”.
